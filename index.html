Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel or switch my plan anytime?

Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. If you downgrade, your current subscription will remain active until the end of your billing cycle.

What does "Yearly Billing Save 15%" mean?

When you select Yearly billing, you pay for 12 months in advance at a 15% discount compared to pay-as-you-go monthly pricing. It is our most cost-efficient option.

Are there any hidden fees or contracts?

No. All our plans are transparent. The price you see is the price you pay, and you can cancel anytime without penalty.

How does the family sharing feature work?

Our Premium plan allows you to invite up to 4 family members living in the same household. Each member gets their own personalized account with all Premium benefits at no extra cost.
